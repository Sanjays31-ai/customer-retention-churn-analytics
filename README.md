# Customer Retention & Churn Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for subscription-based businesses. This project analyzes customer behavior, subscription plans, revenue, engagement, and churn patterns to identify the major factors contributing to customer loss.

The project combines **Python for data analysis** and **Power BI for interactive business intelligence and visualization**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective is to:

- Measure the overall customer churn rate
- Identify customer segments with high churn
- Analyze churn across subscription plans
- Compare churn by contract type
- Analyze acquisition channels
- Understand churn reasons
- Identify high-risk customer tenure groups
- Estimate monthly revenue at risk due to churn
